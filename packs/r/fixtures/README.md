# R independent Golden

32 separately hand-marked cases cover bindings/functions, defaults, namespace and
extraction, named calls, native/custom pipes, formulas, loops, right/super assignment,
backticks, variadics, numeric/missing literals, raw/delimited strings and original
Unicode/CRLF comments. These expectations are independent of CST teacher output.
Golden and Playground never enter training roots. Teacher agreement and neural
Golden agreement are separate evidence; neither replaces natural held-out testing.
