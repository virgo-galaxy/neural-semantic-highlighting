# Independent GraphQL Golden

24 hand-marked cases cover operations, variables, aliases, keyword-spelled fields,
fragments/spreads, directives/definitions, SDL, interfaces/unions/enums/inputs,
extensions, descriptions, numeric and object/list values, Unicode escapes,
CRLF/BOM and original UTF-16 offsets. These visible development probes and the
separately authored Playground examples never enter the training corpus.
Teacher agreement and neural whole-case agreement are different measures.
