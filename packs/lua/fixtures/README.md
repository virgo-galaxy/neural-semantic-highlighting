# Lua independent Golden

32 separately hand-marked cases cover functions/parameters, colon methods, table
and computed fields, declarations, const/close attributes, explicit globals, varargs,
loops/repeat/labels, long strings/comments, shebangs, numbers/operators and original
Unicode/CRLF. Expectations are independent of CST teacher output. Golden and
Playground never enter training roots. Teacher agreement and strict neural Golden
agreement are reported separately from natural held-out testing.
