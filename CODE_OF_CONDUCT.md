# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This code applies to Virgo NSH issues, pull requests, discussions and other
project-managed spaces. It applies to everyone who participates.

## Standards

- Treat people with respect. Constructive criticism and technical disagreement
  are welcome.
- Focus feedback on the work and respect personal boundaries.
- Harassment, discrimination, threats and personal attacks are unacceptable.
  Respect other people's privacy and get consent before sharing their information.

## Reporting

Use [GitHub's private reporting options](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
to report abusive content or behavior. Include relevant links and context.
Keep sensitive information out of public issues and discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, lock conversations, issue warnings or restrict
participation. They consider the severity and context of the behavior and
respect the privacy of the people involved.
