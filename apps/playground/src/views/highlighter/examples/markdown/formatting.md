# Formatting and structure

Use **strong text**, *emphasis*, and ~~previous wording~~.
You can combine **strong and *emphasized* text** in one sentence.

A setext heading
---------------

Escaped markers stay visible: \*literal stars\* and \_underscores\_.
References such as &amp;, &lt;, and &#x2728; preserve their original spelling.

A paragraph with `array.map(value => value.name)` stays source text.
